Bundestagswahl 2025

Sources & method

Final second-vote results for the German federal election of 23 February 2025, covering all 299 constituencies. The official CSV is dated 11 March 2025, 19:34; both source files were downloaded on 13 November 2026.

Reading the map

Colour indicates the party with the largest number of valid second votes in a constituency. CDU and CSU share one colour. It does not indicate an absolute majority or which candidate won the constituency seat. Tooltips show the five parties with the largest second-vote shares.

Height is the number of eligible voters divided by the full constituency area in km², including any water surfaces present in the official polygons. It includes non-voters. The geographic footprint times the density makes volume proportional to electorate size, not to votes for the leading party.

Areas are calculated from the unsimplified official geometry after transforming it from ETRS89 / UTM32 to the equal-area projection ETRS89 / LAEA Europe (EPSG:3035). Heights are strictly linear, without clipping or a positive pedestal. The 3D vertical scale is exaggerated for display and is not physical terrain elevation.

Shared display boundaries are simplified at 80 m tolerance, changing individual footprint areas by at most 0.13%. Heights use the original unsimplified area. Multipart constituencies retain all their components at the same height. The orthographic 3D view foreshortens visible surfaces.

Checks and downloadable data

All 299 constituencies match between boundaries and results. The totals reconcile to 60,510,631 eligible voters and 49,649,512 valid second votes. Party second votes sum to the valid total in every constituency; there are no tied first places.
