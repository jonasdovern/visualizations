Landtagswahl NRW 2022: sources and method

Final second-vote results for the North Rhine-Westphalia state election of 15 May 2022. All 128 constituencies are included. This was the latest completed NRW state election when the map was prepared on 17 November 2026.

Official sources

  • Landeswahlleiter NRW: final election results and all constituencies. Each constituency page links to a semicolon-delimited results file. Counts come from the 2022 second-vote column, not first votes or the 2017 comparison.
  • Official statewide totals, used to check the constituency sums.
  • Official 2022 NRW constituency boundaries, published by Stadt Lünen. The service credits the Ministry of the Interior of NRW and IT.NRW. It contains all 128 statewide constituency polygons, identified by LWKNR.

Reading the map

Colour identifies the party with the most valid second votes in a constituency. It does not identify the directly elected candidate or imply an absolute majority. Tooltips show the five largest party shares; all 29 parties with statewide second votes remain in the downloadable dataset.

Height is eligible voters divided by constituency area in km². It includes people who did not vote and is not a measure of resident population or turnout. The height scale is linear, starts at zero, and is neither clipped nor logarithmic. The vertical exaggeration is 0.014 horizontal-map kilometres per eligible voter/km²; it is a visual scale, not terrain elevation. The footprint uses geographic area, so volume is proportional to the electorate, subject to the small display-boundary changes below. Volume does not represent votes for the leading party.

Areas and display boundaries

The downloaded boundary geometry is in WGS84 (EPSG:4326). It is transformed to the equal-area projection ETRS89 / LAEA Europe (EPSG:3035) before measuring area. Areas include all surfaces in the official polygons, including water. Densities use the unsimplified source polygon areas. An independent ellipsoidal area calculation agrees within 0.000006% for every constituency.

The source contains a self-touching ring in constituency 104 and small overlapping or non-matching shared boundaries. The ring repair changes area by less than 0.000002 m². For display, shared boundaries are cleaned with a 1 cm snapping distance and then simplified together at 25 m tolerance. The maximum combined change in a constituency footprint is 0.047%. These display adjustments do not change the density denominators or any vote counts. Multipart constituencies and polygon holes are preserved. The orthographic 3D view foreshortens surfaces.

Checks and data

  • 128 unique constituency IDs, all matched to results and boundaries.
  • 12,965,858 eligible voters and 7,146,831 valid second votes, matching the official statewide totals.
  • All party second votes sum to the valid total in every constituency and to each party’s statewide count.
  • No tied leading parties: CDU leads in 87 constituencies, SPD in 33 and GRÜNE in 8.
